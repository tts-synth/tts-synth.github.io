Updated CLS Mappings for the paper:: Kinship in Speech : Leveraging Linguistic Relatedness for Zero-Shot

The green coloured labels are updated in the below CLS mappings.

  • The chillu characters in the Malayalam script (‘ൺ’, ‘ൻ’, ‘ർ’, ‘ൽ’, ‘ൾ’) are used in conjunction with other consonants to represent specific sounds.
  • ‘ൺ’ closely maps to the sound ‘nx’ (e.g., ‘ണ’ in ‘പണം’ (panam - money)).
  • ‘ൻ’ is close to the ‘n’ sound (e.g., ‘ന’ in ‘വനം’ (vanam - forest)).
  • ‘ൽ’ and ‘ൾ’ match with ‘l’ and ‘lx’ respectively (e.g., ‘ല’, ‘ള’ in ‘മലയാളം’ (Malayalam)).
  • ‘ർ’ closely maps to the sound ‘r’ (e.g., ‘ര’ in ‘കരി’ (kari - charcoal)).
  • In the Bengali script, the Devanagari grapheme ‘व’ (pronounced as ‘va’) has its nearest equivalent as ‘b’ (pronounced as ‘bo’ or ‘bô’).
